Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when you create an account, including your email address and password. Additionally, we store the financial transactions and category labels you enter to track your expenses.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to provide the core functionality of Spendly: logging your expenses, calculating totals, and generating spending summaries. We do not use your data for marketing or profiling.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely in our database. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share or sell your personal or financial data to any third-party services. If we ever integrate with external tools, we will update this policy and seek your consent.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to request the deletion of your account and data, please contact us at privacy@spendly.example.com.
